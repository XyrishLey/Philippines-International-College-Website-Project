About Us

Welcome to God's College

Philippine International College is an undergraduate and post-graduate level institution that exists to serve the Almighty God. We produce highly qualified and competent ministers and leaders who impact real, living change to countless generations through the power of the Holy Spirit.

This God-centered place of learning generates godly spiritual leaders for our denomination — the Philippine Evangelical Holiness Church — and other mission-oriented organizations worldwide.

Our approach is holistic: balancing biblical knowledge, practical ministry, and professional training — all rooted in spiritual formation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply?

Any student called to grow as a Christian leader is welcome to apply — regardless of denomination or background.

What programs are offered?

Undergraduate: BA Asian Studies, BA Theology, Bachelor of Christian Education, Bachelor of Physical Education.

Graduate: MA in Christian Education, MA in Theological Studies, Doctor of Ministry.

How do I apply?

Start by filling out our online application form. Click the "Fill Out Form" button to begin. You may also view the full list of requirements before applying.

Are scholarships available?

Yes! Scholarships are offered based on financial need and spiritual commitment. Contact us for details.

Can international students apply?

Absolutely. We welcome students from around the world who are called to serve through Christ.
