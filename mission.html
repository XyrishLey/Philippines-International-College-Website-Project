What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Every program, every lesson, and every relationship at PIC is anchored in these convictions.

✝

Christ-Centered

Jesus Christ is the foundation of everything we do. Our curriculum, community, and culture are all shaped by the teachings of Christ and a deep commitment to His lordship.

🕊

Spirit-Led

We believe the Holy Spirit is the source of wisdom, power, and transformation. All activities — instruction, worship, and service — are Spirit-led and Spirit-empowered.

📖

Biblical Foundation

The Word of God is our absolute authority. We are committed to rigorous study of Scripture as the foundation for all knowledge, practice, and leadership.

🌏

Global Vision

We train leaders not just for the Philippines but for the world. Our graduates are equipped to serve across cultures, denominations, and nations with the gospel of Christ.

🤝

Servant Leadership

Following Christ's example, we cultivate leaders who serve with humility, love, and sacrifice — placing the needs of others above their own ambitions.

⭐

Excellence

We pursue the highest standards in academic scholarship, ministry practice, and personal character — all as an offering of worship to God who deserves our best.
