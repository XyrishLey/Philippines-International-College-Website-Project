Our History

Tracing Philippine International College from its humble beginning as EHIBI to a Christ-centered college with a global mission.

"Tracing our school's journey year by year through faith, service, and God's guidance."

Our Roots

A Journey of Faithfulness

Philippine International College began on June 10, 1996 as the Evangelical Holiness International Bible Institute. From a small Bible institute, the school grew through prayer, leadership, government recognition, academic expansion, and international partnerships.

Through the years, the institution developed from EHIBI into International Evangelical College and eventually into Philippine International College, embracing its identity as God's College and its calling to train global Christian leaders.

PIC Timeline

History Timeline

Important milestones in the growth of Philippine International College.

June 10, 1996

Founded as EHIBI

The Evangelical Holiness International Bible Institute was established, marking the beginning of what would become Philippine International College.

✦
✝
December 21, 1998

Board of Directors Organized

The Board of Directors for the support and development of the theological school was formally organized, helping establish the school's future direction.

June 1, 1999

PTSU Accreditation

The school earned accreditation as a member of the Philippine Theological School Union, strengthening its credibility in theological education.

🏛
🎓
April 4, 2000

First Graduation Ceremony

The first class of graduates completed their studies, beginning a continuing legacy of academic and spiritual formation.

2001-2003

Continued Graduations

Succeeding graduation ceremonies were held, showing steady growth in the school community and its academic mission.

✦
✦
May 9, 2003

Renamed International Evangelical College

The institution was officially renamed International Evangelical College, reflecting its growing vision and reach.

November 18, 2003

Foundation Registration

The school completed its registration as an educational foundation and received formal recognition from the immigration office.

📄
📍
December 15, 2003

Moved to Antipolo City

The institution transferred to its present campus in Antipolo City, Rizal.

March 23-24, 2004

Consecration and 5th Graduation

The college held its consecration service, followed by its fifth graduation ceremony the next day.

✝
🏛
February 14, 2005

DepEd Four-Year College Recognition

The Department of Education accredited the institution as a four-year college, a major milestone in its academic development.

March 22, 2006

Korean Studies Approved

The Department of Korean Studies was approved, expanding the institution's academic offerings and international direction.

🌏
⭐
September 5, 2006

10th Founding Anniversary

The institution celebrated its 10th Founding Anniversary with a proclamation ceremony and renewed vision.

March 21, 2009

World Missions Approved

The Department of World Missions was approved by the Department of Education, strengthening PIC's missional identity.

🌏
🤝
November 13, 2009

Seoul Theological University Partnership

The institution forged a twinning agreement with Seoul Theological University, strengthening international linkages.

June 24, 2010

Jeonju University Partnership

A partnership with Jeonju University was formalized, followed by a student exchange program later that year.

🤝
⭐
June 24, 2011

15th Founding Anniversary

The school celebrated fifteen years of God's faithfulness in education, ministry, and mission.

2012

New Programs and Memberships

The school launched Korean language special courses, formed a twinning partnership with Seo Hai College, and became a member of ACSCU.

📖
🎓
June 3, 2013

Graduate School Opened

The Graduate School officially opened, offering Master's and Doctorate programs for advanced theological and ministry studies.

October 15, 2013

Music and Fine Arts Education Approved

The Department of Education granted approvals for Music and Fine Arts Education.

🎼
✦
April 3, 2014

Officially Renamed PIC

The institution was officially renamed Philippine International College, embracing its identity as God's College with a global mission.

September 15, 2014

Indiana Aerospace University MOU

A memorandum of understanding was signed with Indiana Aerospace University, showing continued institutional development.

🤝
🏛
November 7, 2014

PIC Foundation Established

The PIC Foundation was formally established and the new chairman of the board was inaugurated.
